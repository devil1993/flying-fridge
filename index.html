Flying Fridge is the digital noticeboard that keeps your whole family on the same page — tasks, notices, pantry, lost & found, and open questions, all in one shared space.

Everything your family shares

Five magnets, one board

Each topic gets its own colour-coded space — just like the physical magnets on your real fridge door, but digital, searchable, and always with you.

Tasks

From shopping runs to paying bills — post chores and to-dos so nothing slips through the cracks. Anyone in the family can pick it up and mark it done.

Notices & Info

Heading out early? Working late? Drop an announcement so the whole family knows what's happening, without the "why didn't you tell me?" moment.

Lost & Found

Missing your keys again? Post it and let the family track it down. Others can mark items as found so you stop looking in all the wrong places.

Pantry

Track what's in the fridge and what's running low. Note expiry warnings, leftovers, and quantities so nobody buys milk you already have three cartons of.

Questions

Movies this Sunday — is anyone in? Post open questions to the family and collect replies. It's the digital version of a note left on the kitchen table.

Two ways to see it all

Your board, your way

Switch between a clean list for getting things done and a calendar for spotting what's coming up — same data, different lens.

List view

All your family's posts in a clean, scannable list. Filter by category, assign to a person, and tick things off as they get done.

Up and running in minutes

Three steps to a shared board

No complicated setup, no manual to read. If your family can put a magnet on a fridge, they can use Flying Fridge.

1

Create your account

Sign up in seconds. No app store needed — Flying Fridge lives in your browser, so every device in the house can reach it instantly.

2

Invite your family

Share a simple invite link or code. Each person joins your shared family space — everyone sees the same board, in real time.

3

Start sharing

Post tasks, drop a notice, flag a lost item, update the pantry. Your fridge door just got a lot smarter — and it travels in your pocket.
