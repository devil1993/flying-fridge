This is project flying fridge.
# Overview:
This is a digital noticeboard typically optimized for a family. This consists of some basic features. Users can log into the app and join a family. Where they are able to share tasks, info/notice, lost and found, pantry, questions. They have list and calendar views. The goal is to optimise the general topics in a family that we easily forget. It si called flying fridge because of the typical fridge and magnet ways of sharing data. We use digital and online version of it that gives the 'flying' term. 

Below are the overview of the functionalities.

## Tasks
Obvious - tasks that we need to perfrom as a family. Ex: Shopping, Laundry, Visit someone, Pay Bills etc.

## Info/Notice
Infomration for everyone - example "I have a holiday on 15th." or "I need to go office early on Monday", "I'll have dinner outside with friends next Friday" etc.

## Lost and Found:
Things I am looking for - "Can't find my black gloves", "2 of my handcarchifs are gone", "Anyone seen my headphones?" - others can mark as found.

## Pantry
What is in the fridge. "Leftover chicken for 2 people", "Rice for 1 and a half", "Breads expire on Monday" and people can mark stuff as consumed/deleted.

## Questions
Other questions than lost and found - "I'll go for movies this Sunday, anyone interested?" - Probably this is the least thought through one.
